# Holdout Squad: story and unlock design notes

## Route so far
- Stage 1: The Outskirts (wall defense). Bruno freed from the jeep (joins lv 2).
- Stage 2: The Escape (truck roof). Rook arrives mid-stage (joins lv 4).
- Fork in the road (first major choice, hidden consequences):
  - Highway -> Stage 3 The Empty Town. Team stays together, Hex is missed (met later as a zombie witch), Blaze auto-recruited at the lab, Volt easier.
  - Backroad -> Stage 3 The Forest. Bruno leaves for the lab. Hex can be recruited via the pin mini-game (joins lv 5). Lab is overrun, Volt harder.
  - If Bruno has fallen, the choice is forced to the backroad.

## Stage 4: Cyber Future Labs (not built yet)
- Town route: arrive at the lab, Blaze joins automatically. Survivors think it's a safe zone and settle in (they leave the party).
- Forest route: the lab is in chaos. Bruno and Blaze are both on a rescue timer (details TBD). Survivors are scared off by the chaos and retreat in another direction (they leave the party).
- Volt is in an experimental tank in the lab.
  - Town route: hidden secret. Tap the tank to free him, like the Bruno jeep, but NO meter shows. The tank just has an aura so it stands out.
  - Forest route: the tank is surrounded by fire and debris, the aura is fainter. He can only be freed if Hex is in the party: once the hidden meter fills she teleports him out.

## Later
- Frost: joins automatically, not on the stage after the labs but the one after that (stage 6). Boards TBD.
- Nova: last unlockable, very easy to miss. Most likely the stage after Frost. Requires every other hero alive; if anyone is missing, every attempt to help her fails and costs more party members. With everyone alive, heroes can still be lost in the rescue if assigned the wrong duties (duty assignment TBD).
