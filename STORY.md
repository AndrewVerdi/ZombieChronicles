# Holdout Squad: story and unlock design notes

## Route so far
- Stage 1: The Outskirts (wall defense). Bruno freed from the jeep (joins lv 2).
- Stage 2: The Escape (truck roof). Rook arrives mid-stage (joins lv 4).
- Fork in the road (first major choice, hidden consequences):
  - Highway -> Stage 3 The Empty Town. Team stays together, Volt easier. Town ending: the endless swarm chases the squad toward Cyber Future; Blaze (Bruno's wife) steps out ("Care for a light?") and the flamethrower mini-game decides her fate. Win: she torches the horde and joins (if Bruno is dead, she learns he didn't make it). Lose: Blaze is overrun, and if Bruno is alive he charges in and is lost too; the rest slip into Cyber Future as the gates close. Hex is met later: the squad comes upon Momma mid-ritual, casting a spell to turn Hex while her Screecher children circle them singing. If Volt is in the party, he launches chain lightning that takes out Momma's children and breaks the ritual and kills Momma herself in the blast, a showcase of Volt's true power. Hex is saved (she can join). Without Volt, the ritual goes through and Hex is fought as the zombie witch.
  - Backroad -> Stage 3 The Forest. Bruno leaves for the lab. Hex can be recruited via the pin mini-game (joins lv 5). Once Hex joins, she must survive the rest of the forest; losing her ends the run. Forest ending: Cyber Future is burning inside, the guards are gone, screams and roars come from within; the civilians panic and run, half the horde chases them, the rest turns on the squad. With Hex: she's drained from Momma, 10-second tap mini-game to charge a teleport while the Commander and Rook shout at her to hurry. Win: they teleport inside the walls into chaos. Lose: game over. Without Hex (pin mini-game lost): pinned at the wall, Hex watches from the shadows, recognizes the people she turned away, and casts the teleport from the tree line (she never fought Momma), sending the Commander and Rook (if alive) inside. She stays behind and fades into the shadows; she does not join, and later returns as a version of Momma. Lab is overrun, Volt harder.
  - If Bruno has fallen, the choice is forced to the backroad.

## Stage 4A: Cyber Future (town route)
- Opening cutscene: scientists burst out of the main building into the courtyard; the squad goes in to investigate.
- Almost all side-scrolling, same tower as 4B but with minimal destruction. Playable: Commander, Rook, Blaze, Bruno, plus Volt once freed. Blaze joins the roster here if she won the town ending.
- Scientist meter: scientists run toward the squad to escape. A bullet (or flame, or shotgun blast) drops them; reach them before a zombie and the squad helps them up. A zombie that gets one (always if they're down, sometimes if they're running) turns them. 20 turned (15 in Horde) = game over; 28 scientists in Normal, 34 in Horde. No clock.
- Blaze can run and gun, but it burns her (small, steady self-damage while moving and firing).
- Floor 2: Volt's room isn't blocked; a keypad is visible. Only Blaze's security card opens it (melee with Blaze to swipe). No Blaze, no Volt.
- Floor 3: the brute bursts through the door only when the squad is in front of it, so the hit always lands. Once he's down the squad goes through his door.
- The hall: death everywhere, dense zombies and two brutes, ending at the steel containment doors (the doors from the town ending's last cutscene).
- Containment: the doors burst and 10 brutes spill out at once. View switches behind the team; the player picks 3 heroes plus the Commander, standing in place.
- Ending cutscene: a mass of zombies comes up behind them, too many to fight; they cross the room to a ladder onto a lower roof, see the courtyard destroyed, the main gate torn open by a brute and the horde spilling in. Cyber Future is lost; the squad needs a way out.

## Stage 5: The Forest Run (built)
- Opening cutscene: Frost's radio call, the grappling line over the wall, the zip line into the forest, sneaking toward a ranger station, then screams in the woods.
- Wave 1: Screechers circle the squad, then every regular enemy (grunts, runners, armored, Screechers, 2 Brutes; doubled in Horde).
- Before wave 1 (and the ritual checkpoint), the player picks up to 3 heroes to fight beside the Commander; the rest travel with the squad and count for the story (Volt for the mini-game, Bruno for the lock, everyone at the station).
- The ritual (cutscene, then):
  - Town route with Volt: Volt's mini-game, played inside the cutscene. Hold to charge, let go with the meter in the green, 3 surges before the ritual bar fills. Win: Hex is saved and joins. Lose: fight Momma, turned Hex and Screechers.
  - Town route, no Volt: fight Momma, turned Hex and Screechers.
  - Hex in the squad (either route): the Screechers are reviving Momma. With Volt: the same mini-game; win and Momma stays dead, lose and fight Momma reborn (1.5x health, enraged from the start) with Screechers. No Volt: straight to Momma reborn.
  - Forest route, Hex never joined: fight turned Hex and Screechers.
  - Turned Hex: floats in, throws green orbs that stun, blinks around, calls Screechers. Killing Momma doesn't kill her. If she's defeated, she's gone for good.
- The ranger station (side-scroller):
  - With Bruno in the squad: he shoots the padlock, the noise brings the horde, the squad holds the jeep. One key to find: the jeep key.
  - Without Bruno: the garage stays locked and no noise draws the horde; zombies still find them, in smaller groups (about half as many outside, fewer through the windows). The squad holds the garage. Two keys to find: the jeep key and the garage key. Clock: 8:00 in either mode.
  - Squad of three or more: pick two to go to the station; everyone else holds the jeep or garage. Clock with Bruno: 5:00 Normal, 3:00 Horde.
  - Squad of two with Bruno: Bruno holds the jeep, the Commander goes alone, 1:00 in either mode.
  - Squad of two without Bruno: both go (one guards, one searches), 8:00, two keys. Commander alone: searches alone, 8:00, two keys.
  - At the door, pick who guards; the other searches inside. The guard and the squad hold for as long as the clock runs.
  - Inside: 8 pieces of furniture, MELEE to search; the keys are in random ones each run. Zombies smash through windows and grab you (you can't move until you shoot or hit them off), and zombies outside block your path: they cost time, not health.
  - Nobody can die at the station. If the clock runs out, the horde kills the entire party: game over.
  - Finding the last key plays the keys-in-the-drawer cutscene, then play resumes. Reaching the garage plays the jeep cutscene and ends the stage.
  - Finding the keys isn't the end: the searcher heads back out the door, rejoins the guard, and both run the keys back to the garage while the clock keeps running. Zombies come mostly from the garage side on the way back.
  - The squad holding the garage fires at any zombie in range and turns to shoot ones coming from behind.
- Ending cutscene: the keys, the jeep bursting out of the garage onto the road to Mega City.

## Stage 6: The Drive (built)
- Seat screen first: every living hero must ride, someone must drive. All seats start empty every time. Placing a hero shows what they do in that seat, but not how good they are there: the rankings are for the player to discover.
- Controls: ◀ ▶ steer, GAS, BRAKE (stops only), REV (reverse). Keyboard: arrows/WASD, Down = reverse. The seated heroes aim and fire on their own.
- Route, continuous along one road: forest dirt road → back road → on-ramp → highway → the overrun base → the road to Mega City → the city grid. Roughly 3–4 minutes of driving.
- Small zombies (grunts, runners, screechers) are run over above ~95 speed and chip the jeep a little. Armored and Brutes ram the jeep hard; zombies that reach it bite until shot off. Wrecks, sandbags and trucks litter the road; hitting walls fast hurts.
- The Ward wakes on the highway and chases the jeep for the rest of the stage, rubber-banding to stay close. He slams the jeep when he catches it and throws debris (a red circle warns where it lands). Fill his stagger bar with fire and he's knocked down for about 6 seconds, then gets back up.
- Hex standing: heals the jeep every 7 s and cuts damage 15%. Blaze in the front seat, or standing and firing forward, slowly damages the jeep.
- Mega City: a grid of blocks with barricaded streets; a radar arrow points to Frost's building. Reaching its lobby plays the crash-through cutscene.
- Checkpoints: start, the highway, the city (jeep health carried).
- Losing: the jeep is destroyed → game over in either mode.
- Art (in): top-down jeep (clean / battered under half health / wrecked), dirt, asphalt and base-concrete ground, road props (wrecks, police car, box truck, sandbags, jersey barriers, burning barrels), roadside dressing (pines, guard rails, tents, watchtowers, crates, a dead tank), Mega City rooftops and Frost's helipad tower.
- Top-down Ward (walk / slam / knocked down) and top-down Grunt, Runner, Screecher, Armored and Brute (walk, lunge, flattened corpse).
- Ending is per driver: the mirror shot and full-squad chase exist for every driver; the full-squad lobby crash for all but Rook (falls back to the empty-jeep crash).
- Cutscenes: opening = Frost on the radio → squad boarding → the Commander at the wheel. Ending = Ward in the rear-view mirror (Commander driving) → full-squad chase (full squad + Commander driving) → through the lobby glass (full-squad or empty-jeep version) → the stairwell (full-squad or Commander-alone version).
- Damage was lowered once the art went in; final balance comes later.

## Roadmap: stages 5 to 10 (planned, not built)
- **Stage 5:** distress call from Frost, holed up on a Mega City rooftop in a helicopter; she needs support and a pilot, and knows where a cure might be. The squad fires a grappling hook past the wall, zip-lines away from the horde into the forest, and heads for a ranger station with a vehicle. Town-route players can save and recruit Hex here, only if they have Volt.
  - Screechers appear in Stage 5 on both routes. The squad comes upon a ritual: Momma and a circle of Screechers levitating Hex.
  - Town route, no Volt: Hex is turned. Fight Momma and Hex at the same time.
  - Town route, with Volt: mini-game. Volt charges up and shoots electricity into the water, killing Momma and every Screecher there. Hex falls, the squad gathers her, she joins.
  - Forest route, Hex in the squad: the ritual is ending; the Screechers have revived Momma, stronger and angrier. Fight her.
  - Forest route, Hex never joined: Hex has just been turned. Fight Hex and the Screechers.
- **Stage 6:** driving, from the forest to the back road and onto the highway. The player seats the heroes in the jeep; each seat gives a role and buffs: driver (driving only), front passenger (shoots ahead), two rear seats (support, fire at enemies from the sides), standing in the open back (360° offensive support), sitting facing backward (fires at anything behind). The Ward returns. Ends crashing through the lobby of Frost's building.
  - Any number of heroes can ride, down to just a driver (extremely hard). Each hero has their own benefits per seat (detailed when the stage is built). The Ward shows up partway through and stays a constant threat.
  - Design (given 2026-11-22): all driving, top-down view. Before the stage, the player places heroes in seats. Only the jeep's health matters (hero health isn't shown); if the jeep is destroyed it's game over in either mode.
  - Driver (vehicle maneuverability only), best to worst: Commander, Rook, Blaze, Volt, Hex, Bruno.
  - Front passenger, best to worst: Volt (most damage, chain lightning), Rook (dual pistols, two targets at once), Commander (rate of fire), Bruno (strong shot, enemies must be close), Hex (strong but slow), Blaze (her flame slowly damages the jeep).
  - Two rear support seats, best to worst: Blaze (constant fire keeps enemies back), Rook (dual pistols, quick targeting), Hex (homing, auto-targets the closest threat), Volt (chain goes one direction), Bruno (spray for close enemies), Commander (rapid fire but one direction at a time).
  - Standing, 360° support, best to worst: Hex (constant protection spell, heals the jeep every 7 seconds), Volt (chain lightning in all directions), Commander (360° bullet spray), Bruno (360° spray, strong only up close), Blaze (strong flame, but firing at enemies in front of the jeep damages it), Rook (weak pistol damage).
  - Facing backward, strongest to weakest attack: Bruno, Blaze, Commander, Rook, Hex, Volt.
  - Route, continuous: dirt road out of the forest, the back road, the ramp onto the highway. On the highway the Ward reappears and chases them for the rest of the stage. Then through the overrun base from Stage 1, onto the road to Mega City. Mega City is city blocks with several ways through; some routes lead to Frost's building, and arriving there starts the cutscene of crashing through the lobby.
- **Stage 7:** the player controls Nova, a sniper on a nearby rooftop, covering the squad as it climbs floor by floor on autopilot and covering Frost from zombies. Hidden 6-minute timer to reach the roof and clear it, saving Frost. Saving Nova needs everyone alive, each with a job: Commander preps the helicopter (no time to fly to her); Hex teleports the team over and back; Frost keeps the fires down so the building holds; Volt powers Nova's high-security door and stays at the circuit; Bruno defends Volt; Rook protects Frost; Blaze goes alone to get Nova. If anyone is missing, they thank Nova over the radio, she is overrun, and they fly off.
  - The 6-minute timer is how long the squad takes to reach Frost; it doesn't kill her. She dies only if enemies find her and take all her health. The player's job as Nova is cover fire protecting every party member.
  - If saved, Nova joins and plays in the usual stages, when picked for the party (the cap still applies).
- **Stage 8:** helicopter to a remote island off Mega City with another Cyber Future lab. The squad runs up to the lab.
- **Stage 9:** breaking into the lab. Heroes are assigned to roles, like the Mass Effect 2 suicide mission; the wrong hero in a role makes that part go wrong and someone dies.
  - The squad is paired off and split up depending on the player's strategy; no group is more than 4 deep. Details when the stage is built.
- **Stage 10:** final boss. Then the cure room: the distribution system is broken and must be launched by hand from inside, killing whoever stays.
  - The player picks who stays, anyone including the Commander. That hero is unavailable in the sequel. If the Commander is picked, a random hero takes his place (story reasons). Details when the stage is built.
- **Final cutscene:** the survivors on the island shore watch the chemical spread, killing the undead, the mutated and the virus. A massive alien ship appears overhead; the team is beamed up and the ship leaves.

## Stage 4B: Cyber Future (forest route)
- Wave 1, the courtyard: 100 turned civilians, scientists and soldiers (200 in Horde), a Brute walking with the horde, then two more Brutes as it closes in.
- At the start Bruno radios in: he and Blaze are pinned on the third floor. A clock starts (5:30 Normal, 6:40 Horde) and keeps running through the tower.
- Inside: side-scroller. Drag to move, hold SHOOT, tap MELEE, tap a portrait to switch heroes. No healing, no abilities.
  - Floor 1: head right to the stairs, 25 grunts from ahead.
  - Floor 2: head left, grunts from both sides. Volt is trapped in the Power Lab behind debris and fire ("ARGH!"). Hex's melee clears the debris, then Rook's or the Commander's melee kicks the door in. No hint is given. Volt joins.
  - Floor 3: grunts walk toward Bruno and Blaze's barricade; ~20 attack it and peel off when shot. Bruno and Blaze give cover fire. With a few grunts left a Brute bursts through a door. Volt is best against it, then Hex, the Commander, Rook; Rook's blade is best up close.
- The clock stops when the squad reaches the fight. If it runs out first, the barricade falls and Bruno and Blaze are lost for good.
